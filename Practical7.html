SBJITMR student help portal

Your questions, answered.

Find helpful information about life and learning at SB Jain Institute of Technology, Management and Research.

Visit the institute's official admissions page to review eligible programs, required documents, and application dates. Submit the completed application through the instructed admissions process and keep your application details for future reference.

Check the student portal or your department notice board for the latest timetable, classroom details, and academic announcements. Contact your department office if a timetable entry needs clarification.

Follow examination notices from your department for registration dates, schedules, seating arrangements, and required documents. For a correction or special request, contact the examination cell before the deadline mentioned in the notice.

Students can use the institute's academic and campus facilities according to department schedules and campus guidelines. Ask your department office about access to laboratories, the library, student activities, and other available services.

Keep your mark sheets, identity proof, photographs, transfer certificate, and category or domicile documents ready when applicable. Check the current admission notice for the complete program-specific list.

Use the fee payment option in the student portal or follow the payment instructions issued by the institute. Save the receipt or transaction number after completing the payment.

Carry your valid student identity card to the library circulation desk, search for an available title, and follow the borrowing limit and return period set by the library.

Contact the institute administration or hostel office for current availability, eligibility, fees, application dates, and the hostel rules applicable to students.

Review scholarship announcements from the institute and complete the relevant application before its deadline. Submit the required income, academic, and identity documents to the designated office.

Students can collect their identity card from the library after completing the institute's issuing process. The card serves as both your student identity card and your library access card, so keep it safe and carry it whenever you visit the library.

Watch department and campus notices for club registrations, events, and student activity meetings. Contact the activity coordinator or student representatives to learn about available groups.

Contact the campus IT or computer laboratory support team through the department office. Include your student ID, a short description of the issue, and a screenshot when possible.

Still need help? Contact the SBJITMR department office during its posted hours.
